# Toastmasters branding in these skills

Every skill that produces a page, deck or document follows these rules. Copy this section into a
new skill rather than linking to it: a skill is installed on its own and can't read this file.

## Colours

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loyal Blue | `#004165` | Primary: headers, title bars, main chart series |
| True Maroon | `#772432` | Accent: section rules, emphasis, "needs attention" |
| Cool Gray | `#A9B2B1` | Secondary: borders, base/target marks, muted text on dark |
| Happy Yellow | `#F2DF74` | Highlight only, sparingly |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Backgrounds |

- Never put white text on Happy Yellow or Cool Gray; use Loyal Blue or black.
- Good and bad states must not rely on colour alone; add a label or icon.

## Type

- Headings: **Montserrat**. Body: **Source Sans 3**. Both are on Google Fonts and stand in on the
  web for the brand's print typefaces.

## Logo and marks

- Don't draw, recreate, recolour, stretch or animate the Toastmasters logo, and don't build a new
  mark from it.
- Use a logo only when the user supplies their district's approved file, placed unaltered with
  clear space around it.
- Without a logo, the title text is the brand.

## Footer and disclaimer

Every page and slide ends with:

> Data as of <date> · Source: Toastmasters daily reports via My Toastmasters Dashboards · Not an
> official Toastmasters International publication.

## Voice

Encouraging and specific: recognise wins first, then name what needs attention with a next step
and a date. Reports are read by volunteers, so describe clubs' situations factually and never
shame a club or officer.

For the full rules, check the current Toastmasters brand manual on the Toastmasters International
website.
